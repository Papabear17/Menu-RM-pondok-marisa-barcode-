## 2025-10-27 - Accessible Icon-Only Buttons in POS Checkout
**Learning:** Screen readers and keyboard navigation users frequently struggle with icon-only buttons as they completely lack accessible text descriptors, causing navigational bottlenecks in fast-paced interfaces like POS terminals.
**Action:** Always provide explicit `aria-label` attributes for icon-only action elements (e.g., sidebar toggles, cart clear buttons, and draft modal openers) to ensure structural elements are perfectly announced by screen readers.
