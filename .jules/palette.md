## 2023-11-15 - Enhancing Cashier Web App Accessibility and Keyboard Operability
**Learning:** Static POS cashier systems often rely heavily on rapid mouse clicks or touch-screen interactions, neglecting keyboard navigability and accessibility options. For users with motor impairments or those who prefer quick keyboard navigation, having focusable cards with Enter/Space activation, and keyboard escape handlers to close modals is crucial for speed, ease of use, and overall inclusivity.
**Action:** Always provide `role="button"`, `tabindex="0"`, clean Tailwind focus indicator rings, and active keydown event listeners (for Space/Enter/Escape) when converting custom grid elements or cards into clickable interactive areas. Also ensure all icon-only control buttons have explicit `aria-label` attributes for screen readers.
