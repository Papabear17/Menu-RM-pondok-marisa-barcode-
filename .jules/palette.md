## 2025-08-20 - Accessible Icon-Only Buttons in Vanilla HTML POS
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons in vanilla HTML/Tailwind POS web apps (such as drawer toggles, modal closes, and cart quantity adjusters) lack default accessible names. Adding explicit `aria-label` attributes ensures screen reader compatibility without visual or styling side effects.
**Action:** Inspect all icon-only interactive elements in HTML templates and attach descriptive, localized `aria-label` attributes.
