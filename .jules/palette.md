## 2025-05-18 - Accessibility Improvement for Icon-Only Buttons
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons without `aria-label` or `title` attributes prevent screen readers from announcing their purpose to visually impaired users. Adding descriptive `aria-label` attributes improves accessibility significantly without affecting visual design.
**Action:** Always check interactive icon-only buttons across templates and ensure `aria-label` and `title` attributes are present.
