## 2026-11-03 - Accessible Destructive Actions in POS Cart
**Learning:** In fast-paced Point of Sale (POS) interfaces, icon-only buttons for destructive operations (such as resetting an active order) can be triggered accidentally, wiping away cart contents before saving or checkout. Furthermore, icon-only buttons require explicit `aria-label` and `title` attributes so that screen readers and hover states communicate their destructive nature. Adding a conditional confirmation dialog that only triggers when items are present preserves quick checkout flows while preventing data loss.
**Action:** When working on cart/order management views, guard destructive clear actions with active cart checks, allow programmatic overrides (`force=true`) for checkout completion, and ensure icon-only buttons include descriptive `aria-label` and `title` attributes.
